About Us

Where Foundation Meets Ambition

Backed by a strong legacy of SVKM's 30+ educational institutions pan India, the Mukesh R. Patel Military School aims to build strong character based on six pillars — self-awareness, self-discipline, trustworthiness, self-respect, responsibility, and empathy.

This residential school (SSC Board), inaugurated in 2003, prepares boys and girls physically, mentally, and emotionally to join the Defense services and provides students with the resilience to face the challenges of life. We focus on preparing students with moral values, problem-solving skills, and developing healthy living choices.

Why Choose Us

Building Tomorrow's Leaders

Our unique blend of military training and academic excellence shapes students into disciplined, confident, and capable individuals.

Military Discipline

Structured routines, drills, and obstacle courses that instill punctuality, responsibility, and unwavering discipline in every cadet.

Quality Education

Experiential learning with low student-teacher ratios, modern labs, and project-based curriculum aligned with SSC Board standards.

Physical Training

Comprehensive sports programs, fitness routines, and obstacle courses that develop teamwork, leadership, and physical resilience.

Experienced Faculty

Dedicated teachers and coaches who mentor each student individually, focusing on academics, character building, and personal growth.
